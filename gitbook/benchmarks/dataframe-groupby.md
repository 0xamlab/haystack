# Dataframe group-by

The H2O db-benchmark groupby suite is the standard public comparison for dataframe group-by performance. This page runs questions q1–q5 and q7 of the 0.5 GB tier — 10,000,000 rows, K=100 — through the query plane and times Polars and DuckDB on the same data, on the same machine, in the same session.

The Algenta column is `group_by_many` in `query.aggregate`: the single shape-dispatched entry point, which selects its internal engine from the shape of the request. No per-question engine choice is made by the caller, and the benchmark gates the dispatcher against the best explicit engine per question before any number is reported.

## Results

Wall time in seconds, minimum of six repetitions per engine per question. Ratio is Algenta over the named baseline; below 1.00 means Algenta is faster.

| Question | Algenta | Polars | DuckDB | vs Polars | vs DuckDB |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| q1 — sum(v1) by id1 | 0.0070 | 0.0044 | 0.0036 | 1.61 | 1.98 |
| q2 — sum(v1) by id1, id2 | 0.0556 | 0.0570 | 0.0130 | 0.98 | 4.29 |
| q3 — sum(v1), mean(v3) by id3 | 0.0450 | 0.0351 | 0.0424 | 1.28 | 1.06 |
| q4 — mean(v1), mean(v2), mean(v3) by id4 | 0.0185 | 0.0069 | 0.0052 | 2.70 | 3.53 |
| q5 — sum(v1), sum(v2), sum(v3) by id6 | 0.0537 | 0.0397 | 0.0488 | 1.35 | 1.10 |
| q7 — max(v1) − min(v2) by id3 | 0.0448 | 0.0375 | 0.0456 | 1.19 | 0.98 |

Algenta is ahead of Polars on q2 and ahead of DuckDB on q7. It is behind DuckDB by 1.06x on q3, 1.10x on q5, 1.98x on q1, 3.53x on q4 and 4.29x on q2, and behind Polars on every question except q2. The per-question table is the result; no average across questions is published.

## Method

* **Dataset.** The H2O db-benchmark G1 groupby data at the 0.5 GB tier: 10,000,000 rows, K=100, generated by a seeded, deterministic generator following the official datagen spec. Group cardinalities are 100 (id1, id4), 10,000 (id1 × id2) and 100,000 (id3, id6). The original benchmark's categorical columns are emitted as integer dictionary codes for every engine equally: Polars and DuckDB receive the codes as native int64 columns, while the columnar plane stores the same codes as Float64.
* **Timing.** Minimum of six repetitions per engine per question — two cycles of three, all engines interleaved in the same session. Every engine aggregates in-memory columns; no CSV parse is timed. The load average is recorded around every timing block; it ranged from 8.4 to 10.3 on a 16-core machine during the published run.
* **Versions.** Polars 1.43.1 and DuckDB 1.5.5, both at their default parallelism.
* **Hardware.** Apple M4 Max, 16 cores, 128 GiB.
* **Correctness.** Per question, a checksum — the sum over groups of every aggregated column — is compared across all three engines: exactly equal on the integer aggregates (q1, q2, q7) and equal within floating-point summation order on the continuous column (q3, q4, q5 agree to better than 2e-14 relative). Algenta's internal engines are additionally asserted against each other in-process, so a faster wrong answer fails the run rather than shipping.
* **Semantics.** Group-by results preserve first-appearance order — the aggregate columns of q3, q4, q5 and q7 align without a join. NaN values count and contribute to no extremum: a NaN row is counted in its group, and a NaN never becomes a group's min or max.

## Scope

* **One tier measured.** These results are the 0.5 GB tier (10,000,000 rows). The 5 GB and 50 GB tiers of the suite are not yet measured.
* **q6, q8 and q9 are out of scope.** Grouped median (q6), largest two per group (q8) and grouped correlation (q9) require grouped order statistics and grouped co-moments, which the query plane does not provide. They are omitted rather than approximated.
* **Losses are stated above.** Algenta is behind DuckDB on every question except q7 — by 1.98x on q1, 4.29x on q2, 1.06x on q3, 3.53x on q4 and 1.10x on q5 — and behind Polars on every question except q2.
* **Not comparable to the public leaderboard.** The official db-benchmark groups by string keys; here the categorical columns are integer dictionary codes for every engine equally. These timings compare the three engines against each other on this data and do not reproduce published leaderboard entries.
* **Timings are machine-specific.** The absolute seconds hold for the hardware named in [Method](#method); the ratios are the result.

## Reproduce

`benchmarks/h2o_groupby/generate.py` writes the seeded dataset, and `benchmarks/h2o_groupby/run_baselines.py` times Polars and DuckDB on the same columns and prints the matching per-question checksums. The Algenta side runs from the repository:

```text title="benchmark"
mojo run benchmarks/mojo_benchmarks/bench_h2o_groupby.mojo
```

The benchmark asserts its cross-engine checksums in-process and gates the dispatcher — the routed entry point must stay within 5% of the best explicit engine on every question — before printing its result. Gates check properties, not absolute times, which vary by machine and load.
